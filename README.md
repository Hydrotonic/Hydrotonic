Hydrotonic is an agricultural technology startup from Belagavi, Karnataka. We design and manufacture affordable hydroponic tower systems that grow fresh vegetables in limited space, and we stay with our customers from choosing a system to their first harvest.

Founded in 2025 by Mohammed Daanish Mulla, B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, UAS Dharwad.

### What we offer

- **Hydroponic tower systems:** vertical towers designed and manufactured in-house, sized for a balcony, terrace, rooftop or commercial setup.
- **Inputs and accessories:** nutrients, cocopeat, net pots, seedling trays, pumps and fittings.
- **Farm setup:** planning and installing hydroponic growing facilities.
- **Growing support:** practical guidance on crops, nutrients, pests and disease.
- **Fresh produce:** locally grown leafy greens, microgreens and specialty crops.
